If you are building event pipelines, metrics dashboards, or log analytics, there is a good chance ClickHouse sits at the end of the line. It is fast, column-oriented, and built for append-heavy workloads. The question for Camel users is usually not whether to use ClickHouse, but how to connect to it cleanly from routes.
