On March 19, 2007, at 10:54 UTC, James Strachan pushed commit 77b260b6 with the message “Initial checkin of Camel routing library.” It contained 42 files across two modules: camel-core and camel-jms. Nineteen years later, the repository has crossed 100,000+ commits from 1,600+ contributors, ships 350+ integration components, and runs in production from CERN’s Large Hadron Collider to UPS processing tens of billions of messages per day.
