Apache Camel has fixed 7,070 out of 7,081 reported bugs — a 99.8% resolution rate — with a median fix time of 1 day. That track record spans 19 years, 350+ connectors, and 272 production releases. This is not a recent improvement — the project has maintained a 1-day median resolution for 17 of the last 19 years.
