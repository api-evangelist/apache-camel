Apache Camel has always excelled at connecting systems, but observing what happens inside your running integrations has traditionally meant switching between CLI commands, log files, or firing up a web console. With Camel 4.21, we are introducing something new. Camel TUI is an interactive terminal dashboard that gives you a live view of your Camel integrations as they run on your local machine.
