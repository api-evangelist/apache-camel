Apache Camel 4.23 has just been released. This is the biggest Camel release in a long time. Since 4.22 we have resolved 1030 JIRA tickets (nearly twice as many as in 4.22): 503 bug fixes, 362 improvements and 53 new features, made in over 2000 commits by 63 contributors.
