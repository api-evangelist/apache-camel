Apache Camel 4.22 LTS has just been released. This is a Long Term Support (LTS) release, which means it will receive patch releases with bug fixes and security updates for approximately one year. The supported LTS lines are now 4.18.x and 4.22.x (4.14.x reaches end of life with this release).
