What if your AI assistant could do more than answer questions—what if it could actually work with your business systems? Imagine asking an AI assistant to summarize previous customer interactions, highlight open topics, or record a meeting note directly in your CRM. No switching between applications, no copying data between screens, and no need to develop a separate AI application for each business operation.
